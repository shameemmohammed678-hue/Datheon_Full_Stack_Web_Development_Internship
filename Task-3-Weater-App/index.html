Atmos - Weather Inteligence

Atmos is a real-time weather application designed to keep you informed about the weather anywhere in the world. Powered by the OpenWeatherMap API, it provides current weather conditions, temperature, humidity, wind speed, atmospheric pressure, and five-day forecasts. With dynamic weather visuals, an intuitive interface, and responsive design, Atmos makes exploring weather conditions simple, seamless, and enjoyable.
